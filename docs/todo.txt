Additional macro concerns from overview section

[A1] SUTVA / Medicaid spillovers in ACA application
  - State-level treatment, county-level outcomes. Border counties may see
    spillovers via provider networks, patient migration, federal funding
  - [ ] Cite Medicaid spillover literature (e.g., Baicker et al.)
  - [ ] Add border-county robustness: drop counties within X miles of an
        expansion/non-expansion border, or test whether control counties
        near the border show differential trends
  - [ ] At minimum: 1-paragraph discussion of why spillovers unlikely to
        drive the sign reversal

[A2] Staggered adoption handling unclear
  - Montana (2016), Louisiana (2016), and others expanded after 2014.
    Paper defines treatment as single 2014 event
  - [ ] Document how late expanders are handled (treated-with-delay,
        dropped, or always-control?)
  - [ ] Sensitivity: drop late expanders entirely and see if estimate
        changes
  - [ ] Cite Clarke et al. (2023) staggered SDID explicitly

[A3] Single weight specification — robustness check needed
  - Paper's empirical evidence rests on one weight vector (population)
  - [ ] Robustness table: weighted SDID under
        (i)   population (baseline)
        (ii)  trimmed population (cap top 5% counties)
        (iii) inverse variance weights
        (iv)  propensity-score-derived weights (if available from DLPS rep)
  - [ ] Report whether the -13.5 estimate survives; decompose contribution
        of top-10 counties to the weighted ATT

[A4] Worked parametric example showing when weighting flips the sign
  - Central claim is that weighting can reverse sign; a transparent algebra
    example would make this concrete
  - [ ] Two-treated-unit example under simple interactive FE model:
        unit 1 weight 0.9, tau_1 = -15; unit 2 weight 0.1, tau_2 = +10
  - [ ] Compute tau^sdid and tau^w explicitly; show the arithmetic
  - [ ] Place in §2.2 as motivating illustration (before ACA application)

[A5] Diagnostic criteria for when to use weighted SDID
  - Paper tells practitioners weighting matters; doesn't tell them how to

  - [ ] Propose a Hausman-style test: compare tau_sdid and tau_tilde_w,

  - [ ] Add a short "When to use weighted SDID" box in §4 (conclusion) or

[A6] Comparison to heterogeneity-robust DiD (Callaway-Sant'Anna et al.)

  - [ ] Add 1-paragraph positioning section: what weighted SDID offers
